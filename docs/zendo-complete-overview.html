Zendo complete platform overview

This document describes the software in the repository. It is not a price, a forecast, a security certificate, or a legal opinion. Contact zendo2026@proton.me.

Zendo is one web application that runs a ledger, a wallet, a meme-coin launchpad, staking, governance, and an explorer. ZendoCoin on that ledger is not the same object as an ERC-20 that a founder may later deploy on Polygon. Publishing the website does not list ZendoCoin on an exchange, does not airdrop the public allocation, and does not deploy a Polygon contract by itself.

1. Introduction — what Zendo is

Zendo is a unified application for one operator-run chain. Inside that application a person can create a wallet, hold ZendoCoin, create and trade meme coins, stake, vote, and read the block log. Those pieces share one process and one state file. They are not five separate products that call Ethereum or Solana for balances.

The pieces are:

  • The Zendo ledger, chain id zendo-1, stored in data/state.json on the machine that runs the app.
  • ZendoCoin (ZCOIN), 18 decimals, base supply 1,000,000,000. It pays gas, staking bonds, votes, and the 0.2 Z fee to create a meme coin.
  • A meme-coin launchpad. Each new coin has its own 6-decimal supply and trades against ZendoCoin on a constant-product curve, then in an in-app pool after graduation.
  • Wallets whose addresses start with znd1. Signup shows a 12-word phrase once. The server stores a hash of that phrase, not the words.
  • Governance proposals and treasury grants, both weighted by liquid ZendoCoin plus delegated stake.
  • Staking to named validators, with an 8% annual reward before commission, paid only when a delegator claims.
  • Portfolio rows in the wallet: spendable ZendoCoin, meme-coin balances valued at the curve or pool spot price in Z, and pending stake rewards.
  • Live screens that refresh coin lists, quotes, and chain status while the process is running.

A separate Rust program, zendo-node, can run beside the website. It keeps its own ledger. The website wallet does not send transfers through it.

The fragmentation problem, and the limit of this answer

Using cryptocurrency today often means a wallet extension, a different site for launching a token, another site for a chart, and another chain’s gas token. Zendo puts the wallet, the launch board, the explorer, staking, and votes in one interface, and it prices meme coins in its own coin so the app does not depend on another chain’s RPC for those balances.

That unification is real inside this process. It does not remove the need for a person to obtain ZendoCoin before they can trade. A new signup starts at 0 Z. The faucet pays nothing. The 649,280,000 Z labeled “Public allocation” sits in one system account and is not sent to new users when the site opens.

Custody, fiat, and licenses

The design intent is that Zendo does not run a fiat till. There is no card charge, no bank payout, and no dollar balance in the ledger. The 12-word phrase is shown once and is not written to the database or to logs. Recovery with the same words restores the same znd1 address, the same balances, and the same stake and vote weight.

The balances themselves live in the chain file on the operator’s disk. A logged-in session can send, trade, and stake. That is an account on this chain, not a second custodial wallet sitting beside the chain. Polygon tokens, after a real deployment, move only when the holder signs an Ethereum transaction in their own wallet. This document does not conclude that an operator needs no money-transmitter or similar license. That question depends on what the operator does with the treasury and the public-allocation account, and on the law of the place they operate. Nothing here is legal, financial, or investment advice.

2. The Zendo app — features, workflows, interface, and stack

Wallet creation

Signup collects a display name (2 to 32 characters), an email, and a password of 8 to 72 characters that contains a letter and a number. The person must accept the terms. The server draws 12 words from the BIP39 English list, returns them once in the signup response, and stores only sha256("zendo-seed:" + phrase). Three quiz words are checked against hashes of the form sha256("zendo-quiz:" + phraseHash + ":" + index + ":" + word). After a correct quiz those quiz hashes are cleared. The person then sets a 4-digit PIN. The pending signup expires in 30 minutes. Eight wrong quiz answers discard it.

The address is znd1 plus the first 38 hex characters of sha256("zendo-wallet:" + phrase). The same phrase always produces the same address. The new account’s ZendoCoin balance is 0.

Sign-in, reset, and recovery

Sign-in is the password, then the PIN. Five wrong PINs pause entry for five minutes. The session cookie lasts 7 days. The PIN gate lasts 10 minutes. Forgot Password does not send email. If the account exists, the reset link is shown on screen and stored for the operator outbox. It lasts 30 minutes and changes the password only. Recover Account asks for the email and the 12 words, replaces the password and the PIN, and keeps the address, balances, and stake. Changing the password or recovering bumps the session so older cookies stop working.

What a signed-in person can do

  1. Open the wallet and read spendable ZendoCoin, meme-coin balances valued in Z, pending rewards, the receive address, and a QR code.
  2. Send ZendoCoin or a meme coin to any znd1 address. An unknown address is recorded on inclusion. The fee is 0.001 Z.
  3. Stake at least 1 Z plus 0.001 Z to an active validator, or start unbonding. Unbonding returns the coins after 2 blocks, about 10 seconds at the 5-second block time.
  4. Claim staking rewards. The claim is the moment new ZendoCoin is minted.
  5. File a governance proposal (1 Z, at least 100 Z of voting power) or vote yes, no, or abstain (0.002 Z).
  6. Create a meme coin for 0.2 Z, then buy and sell it.
  7. Reply on a coin thread, upvote, report, or, if they created the coin, promote it for 50 Z for 24 hours.
  8. Apply for a treasury grant of 1 to 5,000 Z, or publish a marketplace note for 1 Z.

Screens and navigation

The public header links Launchpad, Explorer, and ZCoin, plus Company and Legal menus. Search accepts a block height, a transaction or block hash, a znd1 address, or a ticker. On a phone, the signed-in bar is Home, Launch, Send, Stake, and Govern. Desktop adds Explorer and Settings. An admin also sees Operator.

The field is dark ink with lime and gold type. Coin pages show a sparkline of recent trade prices, a graduation bar, a quote, and one-tap Buy or Sell after the amount is entered. Quotes refresh from the same pricing function the trade will use. Charts are the recent trade prints, not a candle engine and not a dollar chart.

Notifications are in-app. Examples include wallet ready, transfer included, a coin going live on the curve, a grant payout, and a referral join. There is no push service and no mail server.

Security model, as implemented

  • The raw 12-word phrase is not stored and is not written by the logger. The long-term secret on disk is the phrase hash, plus bcrypt hashes of the password and PIN.
  • Identifiers and transaction hashes use the operating system’s cryptographic random bytes, not a weak userspace generator.
  • Mutating actions require the session. They are not individually signed by the phrase on the phone. Someone who steals the session cookie can act as that account until the session ends or the password is reset.
  • Production refuses a public request that is not HTTPS. Localhost health checks stay open. Cookies are marked secure in production or when ZENDO_SECURE_COOKIES=1.
  • Public JSON-RPC is 60 calls a minute, or 600 after an operator marks the account premium.
  • One process owns the ledger file. A second writer on the same file can corrupt it. Containers and the Kubernetes manifest are set to one replica.

3. ZendoCoin — supply, use, price, and Polygon

Role on zendo-1

ZendoCoin is the native coin of the in-app ledger. It is not created on the launchpad. Ticker ZCOIN is reserved so a meme coin cannot take that symbol. Decimals are 18. The base supply is 1,000,000,000. After genesis, the only new coins are staking rewards minted at claim time. Fees move existing coins into the treasury. They do not mint.

Uses: gas on transfer, trade, vote, proposal, stake, and coin creation; the bond when delegating; voting power (liquid balance plus delegated stake); the 0.2 Z creation fee; the 1% meme-coin trade, which is paid in ZendoCoin.

founders.json is the shared table. The running chain imports it through lib/zendo-founders.ts. The Polygon dry-run reads the same file.

300,000,000 + 700,000,000 = 1,000,000,000. A supply check adds every account balance, every validator self-stake, every delegation, and every unbonding row. That sum must equal the base supply plus claimed staking rewards.

How a balance becomes visible

The wallet shows spendable ZendoCoin with 4 decimal places on the main figure, pending stake rewards, and each meme coin as a token amount plus a Z value. That Z value is the spot price times the holding. Spot price is virtual or pool Z per token. There is no dollar column unless an operator sets an environment reference, and this build does not invent an exchange price. Portfolio value in the app is denominated in ZendoCoin.

How ZendoCoin’s quantity changes, and how meme-coin prices move

ZendoCoin’s coin count grows only when someone claims stake rewards. The gross reward is the bonded amount times 800 basis points times elapsed time, divided by 10,000 times one year. The validator commission, 3% to 7% of that reward depending on the validator, is minted to the validator. The rest is minted to the delegator. Jailed validators stop rewards. This inflation can increase the number of coins. It is not, by itself, a higher price.

The app does not quote ZendoCoin in dollars, euros, or against ETH or SOL. There is no order book for ZendoCoin itself. A rise or fall that a person can watch on a coin page is the meme coin’s price in ZendoCoin. Buys move that price up along the curve. Sells move it down. That quoted price can change on every trade, including seconds after a coin is created.

“Grows in value” is therefore not a promise about ZendoCoin’s dollar price. Demand for a meme coin can raise that meme coin’s Z price. Demand for ZendoCoin as gas and as the quote asset is real only among people who already hold it or who receive it by transfer or grant. Without an outside market, there is no continuous public bid.

What “live” does to ZendoCoin

On localhost and in development, the founder clock stays unset and founder coins stay locked. The first time a production process serves the site over HTTPS on a public hostname, the app writes publicLaunchAt once. The releaser runs immediately and pays 0, because no time has passed. From that stamp: nothing unlocks for 120 days, including the cliff day; at 365 days each founder receives 25% of their own allocation (Louis Conner, Co-founder and President, 50,000,000; Prabhat Agrawal 12,500,000; Pratyaksh Lutare 12,500,000); year 2 is 50% cumulative; year 3 is 75%; year 4 is 100%. The block timer moves unlocked coins from that founder’s vesting address to that founder’s wallet. Nobody presses a claim. The operator wallet does not receive those coins.

The 700,000,000 public allocation is already liquid before that stamp. Going live does not distribute it. A member of the public gets ZendoCoin only if a holder sends it, a passed grant pays 1 to 5,000 Z from the treasury, or they already have at least 1 Z and later claim staking rewards.

Polygon mainnet, as a separate token

Polygon chain id is 137. ZendoCoinLaunch deploys ZendoCoin and ZendoCoinVesting in one transaction and reverts on every other chain. Name is ZendoCoin, ticker is $ZCoin, decimals are 18, supply is 1,000,000,000. There is no mint after construction and no admin key. Transfers do not call the recipient. Louis Conner, Co-founder and President, has 200,000,000 locked for 0x18a0730008034177b40F1172aA5953a37dAE7102. Prabhat Agrawal has 50,000,000 locked for 0xD319679625599303393084671a3AdaB52ea999B3. Pratyaksh Lutare has 50,000,000 locked for 0x3336a52Cd82F01956147aed641F35694a235fbc9. The other 700,000,000 is liquid and is sent to the public allocation wallet.

Nothing is released during the 120-day cliff, including on the cliff day. Then 25% of each founder's own allocation unlocks at each 365-day anniversary for four years. The founder calls release() and receives only their own unlocked coins.

How to deploy it

  1. Open polygon/README.md or the phone archive ZendoCoin-Polygon_Mainnet.zip.
  2. Run node polygon/scripts/deploy.mjs. It prints the plan and does not broadcast.
  3. To broadcast, set an https POLYGON_RPC_URL that reports chain id 137 and DEPLOYER_PRIVATE_KEY, install ethers, and run node polygon/scripts/deploy.mjs --broadcast. Optional POLYGON_ADDR_PUBLIC receives the 700,000,000. If it is unset, the deployer wallet receives them. The script does not print the key. The deployer pays Polygon gas in POL.
  4. After the token address exists, holders use their own Polygon wallet to transfer, approve, or burn. The website session does not sign those transfers.

The in-app znd1 balance and the Polygon balance are not the same coins and do not move together. Bridging them is not implemented.

4. The Zendo ledger — architecture and operation

What kind of chain this is

It is a single-process ledger with an append-style block log. Chain id zendo-1. Block time target is 5,000 milliseconds. A block is labeled finalized after two later blocks. “Proof of history” on a block is the height when that flag is on. It is not a verifiable delay function and not a network of independent validators.

Eight validators start active and take turns: North Arch, Harbor Relay, Cedar Quorum, Lime Hollow, Glass Mesa, Redwood Seal, Kite Span, Orchard Gate. Saltworks and Paper Lantern start pending until an operator approves them. They are rows in the same state file. They do not run on separate machines. The peer table on the consensus screen is a set of generated labels.

user action
  -> authenticate session
  -> debit / credit accounts inside the lock
  -> push transaction
  -> seal a block (up to 40 transactions)
  -> fsync state.json

idle timer, about every 5 seconds
  -> heartbeat transaction from the next proposer
  -> seal a block
  -> release any founder coins the schedule has unlocked
  -> release unbonding that has reached its height
  -> close proposals whose clocks have ended

Addresses, wallets, and balances

User addresses come from the phrase, as in section 2. System addresses come from labels: treasury, faucet, community (the public allocation), studio, each founder vesting lock, each founder spendable wallet, each validator, and each meme-coin mint (coin:{symbol}:{creator}). All of them start with znd1.

A balance is a string of base units on an account. ZendoCoin uses 18 decimals. Meme coins use 6. Sending to an unseen znd1 address creates an account labeled “External wallet” and credits it. That is still an account inside this file, not a payment on another chain.

Transactions and blocks

Transaction types include transfer, coin create, trade, stake, unbonding, reward, vote, proposal, parameter apply, grant, listing, referral, vesting, slash, promote, and heartbeat. A user action produces its block before the HTTP response returns. The block hash covers height, parent, time, transaction hashes, and proposer. The state root hashes every account nonce and balance. The explorer keeps roughly the last 320 blocks.

Staking, jail, and governance state

Delegation debits liquid ZendoCoin and records a stake row. Minimum is 1 Z plus 0.001 Z gas. Unbonding waits unbonding_blocks, which is 2. Rewards accrue in the formula in section 3 and mint on claim. A jail slashes 10% of that validator’s self-stake and 10% of each delegation into the treasury, once, pauses rewards, and refuses new stake. Genesis self-stake is 0.

Voting power is liquid ZendoCoin plus delegated stake. A proposal lasts 5 days, costs 1 Z, and needs 100 Z of power to file. It passes when yes exceeds no and yes plus no is at least 1,000 Z. Abstain is stored and does not count toward that quorum. A second active proposal with the same parameter or the same title is rejected before the fee. A passing vote may change block time, finality depth, trade fee, creator share, staking APR, graduation size, minimum stake, unbonding length, the optional fee skim, and the gas amounts. It cannot change the 1,000,000,000 base supply, the 18 decimals, or the founder amounts.

Launchpad registry

Creating a coin pushes it to the front of the coin list and creates an account for the curve. The mint, symbol, creator, virtual reserves, and later the pool reserves are the registry. Hidden coins drop off the board and cannot be traded. There is no separate smart-contract factory on zendo-1.

When the chain is “on”

The chain is on when this process is running and the state file is readable. Signup, trading, staking, and governance do not wait for a second ceremony. The founder clock is the exception: it stays empty until the first production HTTPS request from a public hostname, then it stamps once. A fresh ledger has no meme coins until an account pays the creation fee.

Fees the chain collects

Every fee below is ZendoCoin credited to the treasury, except the 0.30% creator share of a trade, which is credited to the coin creator.

The same license note in section 1 applies here. Non-custodial intent means no fiat till and no stored phrase. It is not a determination that no license applies.

5. Meme-coin launchpad

Who can create a coin

Any signed-in znd1 wallet with at least 0.2 Z plus enough Z for the first trades. The form requires a name, a ticker of 2 to 8 characters starting with a letter (ZCOIN and ZENDO are reserved), a description of 10 to 280 characters, one emoji from a set of 20, and a hue from 0 to 360. There is no image upload and no website or social-link field. If the balance is too low, the debit fails and no coin is registered.

Step by step

  1. The person opens Create Coin while signed in.
  2. The server checks the ticker is free and the launchpad flag is on.
  3. It debits 0.2 Z from the creator and credits the treasury.
  4. It registers the coin: mint, name, symbol, description, emoji, hue, creator address, time, and curve reserves.
  5. It writes a coin-create transaction and seals a block.
  6. The browser opens the coin page. The curve is tradable immediately. The first trade is what starts the printed chart.

A buy debits gross Z plus 0.0005 Z gas. Only the remaining 99% enters the curve. Tokens out equal old virtual tokens minus k divided by the new virtual Z. If the buy would pass 800,000,000 sold, the engine shrinks the fill and the screen says so. A sell reverses the curve, will not buy back more than has been sold, and fails if the proceeds cannot cover gas. Real reserves are current virtual Z minus 30. The progress bar is that amount divided by 85.

At 85 Z of net deposits, about 793 million tokens have been sold. The pool is then seeded with that real Z and with 1,000,000,000 minus tokens sold (about 207 million tokens). Later trades use the same constant-product formula on the pool. There is no fixed-price mode, no Raydium or PumpSwap listing, and no LP token. The virtual 30 Z never becomes real coins.

Price in Z rises when people buy and falls when they sell, on every fill, before and after graduation. Market cap on the card is spot price times the full 1,000,000,000 supply, including tokens still in the curve or pool. A 24-hour volume figure sums Z traded in that window.

Discovery, threads, and hooks

Sorts are trending, new, graduating, and volume. Trending score is 24-hour Z volume times 8, plus vote score times 6, plus 800 if the coin is inside a paid promotion window, plus 40 if the coin is under 12 hours old, plus 15 if the creator had already attached a referral code. Promotion can be stacked by buying another 24 hours before the window ends. Only the creator or an admin can promote.

Each coin has a holder list (top 8), a thread of up to 100 replies, an upvote, a report (Scam, Spam, Impersonation, Other), and a share link. The engine can store a downvote. The screen sends upvote or clear. Reports go to the operator desk, which can hide the coin.

Chain governance and staking are ZendoCoin-wide. They are not a separate DAO inside each meme coin. A meme-coin upvote is one vote per address, not a stake of ZendoCoin. There is no per-coin staking contract. Someone who wants parameter changes or a treasury grant uses the ZendoCoin governance and grant screens.

Launchpad revenue

The protocol receives 0.2 Z at creation, 0.70% of trade notional, 0.0005 Z trade gas, and 50 Z when a coin is promoted. The creator receives 0.30% of trade notional, paid to their znd1 wallet, and can read the total on the coin page. All of those figures are ZendoCoin, not dollars.

Compared with pump.fun

Both let a person create a fixed-supply token and trade it on a virtual curve until a threshold moves liquidity into a pool. Zendo’s pool stays inside this ledger. Pump.fun’s tokens are Solana tokens that can trade where SOL already has buyers. Zendo has no livestream, no king-of-the-hill race, no image metadata, no outside wallet, and no fiat on-ramp. “Outperforms” is not something this curve can demonstrate against a market it does not reach. The practical difference is that a Zendo coin is immediately tradable by anyone who already holds ZendoCoin on this node, and it never leaves this node.

The launchpad is available as soon as the process is up. An empty ledger also shows the six seeded demo coins. No operator has to enable the board after the founder clock stamps.

The advantage that is actually in the software is concentration: one login reaches a wallet, a launch board, an explorer, staking, and votes, and the operator does not pay Ethereum or Solana gas for those in-app movements. 7. The real-world problem this software fits

The problem that matches the code is narrow. A small group wants one place to host a gas coin, a meme-coin board, wallets, an explorer, votes, and grants, without calling another chain for those balances and without the server storing raw recovery phrases. Fees are written in a config file. Founder coins are in named locks. A new meme coin costs 0.2 Z and trades on reserves a person can read.

It does not, by itself, teach a newcomer who has never held a coin, and it does not give that newcomer ZendoCoin. The signup path is short — phrase, quiz, PIN, wallet — and the balance is zero. Mobile layout and a sideload WebView make the same site usable on a phone. They do not put the app in the Play Store, and they do not make the public allocation claimable.

Publishing the site makes the interface reachable. It does not create an outside market. The phrase about licenses in section 1 still applies: avoiding a fiat till and avoiding stored phrases is a design choice, not a ruling that no license is required, and not a statement that the software is available in every country.

8. Revenue and value, in the units the ledger uses

What the treasury receives

The app does not charge dollars. “How Zendo makes money” in this codebase means how ZendoCoin accumulates in the protocol treasury:

  • Launchpad: 0.2 Z per coin, 0.70% of each trade, 0.0005 Z trade gas, 50 Z per promotion.
  • Chain usage: 0.001 Z per transfer, 0.002 Z per vote, 1 Z per proposal, grant application, or marketplace listing, 0.001 Z per stake.
  • Slashes: 10% of a jailed validator’s self-stake and delegations, once.
  • The optional 0.05% trade bump, if an operator turns it on.

Creators make their 0.30% trade share. Delegators make staking rewards, which increase supply. Those rewards are not protocol profit. Grants pay 1 to 5,000 Z out of the treasury after a vote, so a grant is an outflow.

What is not a revenue line

ZendoCoin does not pay a dividend. The chain does not sell block space for dollars. Polygon deployment does not send a fee back to the zendo-1 treasury. If a Polygon token later trades on an outside market, that trade is not recorded here and is not protocol revenue unless some separate arrangement says so. No such arrangement is in this repository.

How value moves up or down

  • Meme-coin price in Z rises on buys and falls on sells, immediately, on the curve and later on the pool.
  • Graduation changes the reserves the formula uses. It does not guarantee a higher price.
  • Promotion can raise a coin on the trending sort. It does not change the curve price by itself.
  • ZendoCoin coin count rises when rewards are claimed, which can dilute holders if demand does not rise with it.
  • Usage of gas and the launchpad increases the treasury’s Z balance. That is an internal transfer from users to the treasury, not a dollar gain.
  • Governance can raise or lower fees and the staking rate inside the allowed ranges. It cannot mint a new base supply.
  • A Polygon listing could create an outside price. This app will not display that price unless someone builds that connection. It is not built.

If someone converts the 0.70% trade fee into dollars, one billion dollars of protocol fees at that rate would require on the order of 140 billion dollars of trade notional in a year, and a stable dollar price for Z. This ledger has neither that volume nor that price. The fee schedule can support a small board whose treasury grows when existing holders trade. It is not, by itself, a plan that produces billions of dollars.

9. What starts when the app is published

When the process is up on a reachable host, these work without a second switch:

  • Wallet signup, sign-in, password reset, and phrase recovery.
  • The launchpad, including create, buy, sell, reply, report, and promote.
  • Transfers, staking, unbonding, and reward claims.
  • Proposals, votes, and grant applications.
  • The explorer, search, and the status stream (about every 2 seconds).
  • Block production about every 5 seconds, plus an immediate block on each user action.

On the first production HTTPS request from a public hostname, the founder clock stamps and stays stamped. No founder coins move that day. Yearly releases later are automatic. The operator does not press a launch button for that clock.

These do not happen by publishing the site:

  • New users do not receive ZendoCoin.
  • The 649,280,000 public-allocation account is not paid out.
  • ZendoCoin is not listed on an exchange and has no dollar price.
  • The Polygon contract is not deployed. A founder still compiles, sets addresses, funds a deployer with POL, and broadcasts.
  • The Android package does not appear in the Play Store. It is a sideload WebView whose saved node address must point at the host.
  • A host that erases its disk erases every wallet. Serverless disks that disappear are the wrong place for this file.

Before a public host, change the seeded operator password and serve HTTPS. The license question in section 1 remains open. It is not answered by the act of publishing.

10. Top to bottom

  • Browser or Android WebView.
  • Next.js pages: home, launchpad, coin, create, wallet, send, stake, settings, governance, grants, marketplace, explorer, ZendoCoin, learn, onboarding, white paper, contact, operator desk, download.
  • HTTP API under /api for auth, wallet, coins, stake, governance, grants, market, admin, health, a status stream, and JSON-RPC.

  • founders.json and lib/zendo-supply.ts for the 300,000,000 / 700,000,000 split. Louis Conner is Co-founder and President.

  • data/state.json as the only website ledger.

  • Optional zendo-node Rust process, not on the website’s transfer path.

User flows

Signup writes a hash and a znd1 account at 0 Z. Recovery with the same words keeps that account. A transfer moves units inside the file and charges 0.001 Z. A stake locks Z and later mints a reward on claim. A vote writes weight and charges 0.002 Z. A coin create charges 0.2 Z and registers the curve. A buy or sell updates reserves, pays the 1% and gas, and can graduate the coin into the pool. A promotion charges 50 Z and marks the coin for 24 hours. A grant, if it passes and someone calls payout, moves 1 to 5,000 Z from the treasury to the applicant.

Dependencies

The in-app ledger does not call Ethereum, Solana, or Polygon to accept a znd1 transfer. Polygon is used only if a person later configures an RPC and broadcasts the contract. There is no mail provider, no card processor, and no price oracle. The Android download of this PDF depends on the website being reachable from the phone. A localhost address is not reachable from a phone on another network.

Smart contracts

On zendo-1, meme coins and ZendoCoin are ledger accounts, not Solidity. On Polygon, the only contract in this repository is ZendoCoin.sol, described in section 3. It is not deployed by the act of publishing the website.

Revenue flow and value flow

Users spend Z. The treasury and coin creators receive the fees in section 8. Stakers receive newly minted Z when they claim. Founder locks release on the schedule in section 3 into founder wallets, not into the treasury. Meme-coin prices move with trades. ZendoCoin has no in-app dollar price. Outside markets are not connected.

Where this is stronger, and what problem it leaves

It is stronger as a single operator-run board: one host, one fee schedule, one wallet, one explorer, founder locks that do not need a manual claim, and a Polygon contract that can be deployed later without pretending it is the same balance. It does not solve price discovery, fiat access, or distribution of the public allocation. Those gaps are why a new visitor can open the launchpad and still be unable to buy.
